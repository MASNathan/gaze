# Roadmap

Ideas for future work, not available features or release commitments.

- **Bitbucket Cloud:** support scanning public repositories.
- **Homebrew:** publish a tested formula in a `masnathan/homebrew-tap` repository, referencing versioned releases and their SHA-256 checksums. Consider submission to `homebrew/core` separately.
- **Ubuntu:** build `.deb` packages and publish them through a signed APT repository or Launchpad PPA. Inclusion in Ubuntu's official repositories requires separate packaging and review.
